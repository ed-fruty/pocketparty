# Privacy Policy

**4 Players Game Offline**  
Package: `com.frutylabs.pocketparty`  
Publisher: FrutyLabs  
Effective date: October 19, 2026  
Contact: ed.fruty@gmail.com

## Offline gameplay

4 Players Game Offline is a collection of local mini-games for one to four players on the same device. It does not require an online account or an internet connection to play.

## Data collection and sharing

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to the publisher. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, online chat, online multiplayer, or third-party data-collection SDKs. We do not sell or share player data.

## Information saved on your device

The game stores gameplay progress locally, including gold coins, unlocked and equipped skins, difficulty, and music and sound preferences. Player seats are identified by numbers and colors. These local saves are not sent to our servers, and the game does not offer cloud synchronization.

You can remove this local information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes locally saved progress.

## Device permissions

The Android game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. Gameplay does not need network access.

## External links and support

If you choose to open the online privacy policy, your browser connects to GitHub's hosting service. That website's own privacy practices apply to that visit. Opening an email link uses your chosen email application.

If you contact ed.fruty@gmail.com for support, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request and address the issue. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

## Children and families

The game does not ask players to provide their name, email address, date of birth, or other personal information. It does not include player-to-player communication or advertising. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy question.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its privacy practices change. The effective date above will be updated. If a future version introduces data collection, the policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated before that behavior is introduced.
