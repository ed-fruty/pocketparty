# Privacy Policy

**4 Players Game Offline**  
Package: `com.frutylabs.pocketparty`  
Publisher: FrutyLabs  
Effective date: October 19, 2026  
Contact: ed.fruty@gmail.com

## Single-device play and optional NetPlay

4 Players Game Offline is a collection of local mini-games for one to four players. You can play on one device, or choose NetPlay to play with friends on separate devices connected to the same local Wi-Fi network. Neither mode requires an online account or an internet connection.

## Data collection and sharing

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to the publisher. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, chat, internet matchmaking, or third-party data-collection SDKs. We do not sell player data. Optional NetPlay exchanges the limited local-network information described below directly between participating devices.

## Optional NetPlay local-network sessions

When you choose Create Room or Join Room, the game communicates directly with other participating devices on your local network. The host device runs the match. Devices exchange temporary connection identifiers, player seat numbers, equipped skin choices, control inputs, game state, scores, and round/tournament results. Local IP addresses are used to discover rooms and establish connections; a host address may be displayed in the room browser or entered manually. Nearby-room announcements are visible to devices listening on the same local network while a room is open.

The publisher does not operate a matchmaking or game server and does not receive these sessions. Connection information and room membership are used in memory to provide the local session and are not intentionally written to persistent gameplay saves or analytics logs. Match scores and tournament standings may remain in memory on participating devices while results are displayed and until a new session replaces them or the app closes. Your device saves your own earned gold and progress locally; it does not upload your wallet, full skin inventory, or a real-world identity to other players or to the publisher. NetPlay does not provide cloud accounts, cross-device progress synchronization, names, messages, or public profiles.

Use NetPlay with trusted friends on a private network. Local gameplay packets are not end-to-end encrypted. You can stop participating by leaving the room; no background room discovery is performed outside the NetPlay join screen. Leaving or disconnecting ends the session connection, while locally earned progress remains on your device.

## Information saved on your device

The game stores gameplay progress locally, including gold coins, unlocked and equipped skins, difficulty, and music and sound preferences. Player seats are identified by numbers and colors. These local saves are not sent to our servers, and the game does not offer cloud synchronization.

You can remove this local information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the game. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes locally saved progress.

## Device permissions

The Android game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. Single-device gameplay does not need network access. Optional NetPlay uses Android network access permission for direct local-network communication. This Android release uses the INTERNET permission for local-network sockets. It does not use local addresses to determine your geographic location.

## External links and support

If you choose to open the online privacy policy, your browser connects to GitHub's hosting service. That website's own privacy practices apply to that visit. Opening an email link uses your chosen email application.

If you contact ed.fruty@gmail.com for support, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request and address the issue. Please do not send sensitive personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

## Children and families

The game does not ask players to provide their name, email address, date of birth, or other personal information. It does not include freeform chat, messaging, public profiles, or advertising. NetPlay exchanges only the game and connection information described above. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy question.
