# Music

party_ambient.wav is an original relaxed instrumental loop at 78 BPM generated by tools/build_ambient.py for this project. Soft plucked notes, warm chords, bass, and quiet brushed percussion use no external samples. Ringing tails wrap across the loop boundary. The source is mastered to 0.16 RMS with peak headroom, and playback volume remains at -18 dB. Music has a separate saved toggle from sound effects and pauses when the app is backgrounded.
