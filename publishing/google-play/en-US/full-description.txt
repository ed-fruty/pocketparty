One screen. Everyone plays.

4 Players Game Offline brings eight small games to your Android phone or tablet. Play solo, add bots, or choose any mix of up to four human and bot players sharing the same device. No internet connection or online account is needed to play.

NETPLAY WITH FRIENDS

Choose Create Room or Join Room to play on separate devices connected to the same private Wi-Fi. The host chooses games or tournaments and fills unused seats with bots or leaves them empty. Each device keeps its own earned gold. NetPlay needs a local network that allows devices to communicate; no internet connection or online account is required.

CHOOSE YOUR CHALLENGE

Snake Clash: Steer your colored snake, eat pizza to grow, and absorb smaller snakes. Becoming the biggest starts a 10-second hunt. A new biggest snake restarts the timer.

Let's Dance!: One chosen player creates five dance moves. Watch the sequence, then repeat every move in the right order. A solo player copies a virtual dance teacher.

Obby Run: Run automatically, jump over blocks, and duck under bars. Hit an obstacle and you are out. The last runner standing wins.

BurgerTower: Drop buns and toppings onto a plate. Build a tall burger tower with ingredients that slide, tilt, and topple.

Pair Peek: Turn over two hidden cards and find matching pairs. Take turns and try to remember what everyone reveals.

Darkspots: Time your tap as the moving circle overlaps the gold range on a rainbow square. Each hit earns five points.

Star Sprint: Tap your control to race toward the finish line.

Signal Snap: Wait for green, then react. False starts cost points.

BUILD A TOURNAMENT

Pick several games and play them in your chosen order. A clear tournament scoreboard shows every round, newly earned points, animated running totals and tied leaders. Reopen the full table from the cup ceremony. The highest total wins the tournament. Tied champions each raise their own red-diamond cup under falling confetti.

MAKE EACH PLAYER YOUR OWN

Earn in-game gold from wins and tournaments, then unlock and equip player skins including Rabbit, Koala, Panda, and Capybara. Gold and unlocked skins are saved locally on the device.
